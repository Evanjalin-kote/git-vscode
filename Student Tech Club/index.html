TechVerse – Student Technology Club

Welcome to TechVerse, a student community where technology meets creativity. It serves as a platfoem for students to explore emerging in hands-on workshops,hackathons, and coding competitions.

TechVerse is a student-run technology club that brings together tech enthusiasts to learn, build, and innovate through hands-on activities like workshops, hackathons, projects, and industry networking

What TechVerse Does

Hands-on workshops (coding, AI, web, cybersecurity, etc). Hackathons, competitions, and team projects. Guest lectures, mentorship, and placement prep.

Why join

Learn beyond the syllabus with practical tools and projects. Build a portfolio, meet like-minded peers, and access internships/opportunities.

What you can learn

Technical: programming, web/app dev, AI/ML, cybersecurity, Git, cloud, etc.
Career skills: problem-solving, teamwork, presentations, resume/interview prep.

Skills you will learn

  • Python
  • Data Science
  • Cyber Security
  • AL & ML

How to become a club member

  1. Fill registration form
  2. Attend orientation
  3. Choose a technical domain
  4. Join a project team
  5. Start learning
